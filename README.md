# Privacy Policy for UriScan

**Last updated:** October 2026

UriScan ("the app," "we," "our") is a wellness screening app that analyzes urine color and dipstick test strips using your device's camera. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it's used, and — most importantly — what never leaves your device.

## The short version

UriScan processes everything **on your device**. We do not operate any servers, we do not collect analytics, we do not use third-party SDKs, and we do not have any way to see your scans, your health data, or your identity. There is nothing to leak because nothing is transmitted anywhere.

## Data the app accesses

| Data type | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | To photograph urine samples and dipstick test strips for color analysis | Processed on-device only; never uploaded |
| Photo library (optional) | To let you import an existing photo instead of taking a new one | Processed on-device only; never uploaded |
| Apple Health — dietary water (optional) | To log an estimated hydration value based on your scan results, if you choose to enable this | Written to your personal Apple Health store via HealthKit; UriScan does not read or export your broader Health data |
| Scan history (parameters, results, timestamps, and optionally the captured image) | To show your history, trends, and PDF reports | Stored locally on your device only, using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage |

UriScan stores one small app preference (your selected display language) using `UserDefaults`, which stays on your device and is never transmitted.

## Data we do **not** collect

- No account, sign-up, or personal identifiers
- No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No location data
- No network requests of any kind — UriScan has no server backend
- No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party, because none is collected in the first place

## Your control over your data

- **Camera and Photo Library access** can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.
- **Apple Health access** can be revoked at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.
- **Scan history** can be deleted at any time from within the app (Settings → Clear Data). Deleting the app removes all locally stored data immediately, since nothing exists outside your device.

## Children's privacy

UriScan is not directed at children and is not intended to collect data from children. Since the app collects no data off-device in the first place, no children's data is ever received, stored remotely, or shared by us.

## Medical disclaimer

UriScan is a wellness screening tool and is **not a medical device**. It has not been cleared or approved by the FDA or any other regulatory body. Results are for informational and educational purposes only and are not a diagnosis. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding any health concern, and seek emergency care immediately for a medical emergency rather than relying on this app.
